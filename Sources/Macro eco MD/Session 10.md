 What if critical links fail?
Liquidity trap , a situation in which the public is prepared, at a
given interest rate, to hold whatever amount of money is
supplied.
 •   LM curve is horizontal and that changes in the quantity of
money stock do not shift LM
 •   Monetary policy carried out through open market

level of income.
 •   In the liquidity trap, monetary policy is powerless to affect
