 Is Crowding Out Important?
•   So far, we have assumed an Economy in which   price level is
given   and   output is below the full-employment level .
 •   Fiscal expansion increases demand, firms can increase the level
of output by hiring more workers.
 • What if the economy is at full employment level?
 •   In Full employment economy when fiscal expansion happens
 - increase in demand will lead to an increase in the price level
 - increase in price reduces real balances. (M/P)
 - reduction in the real money supply moves the LM curve to
 the left, raising interest rates until the initial increase in aggregate
demand is fully crowded out.

 The Composition of Output and the Policy Mix
•   We look at what those policies do to the components of aggregate demand,
that is, to investment, consumption, and government spending.
 •   Monetary policy operates by stimulating interest-responsive components of
aggregate demand, primarily investment spending.
 •   Fiscal policy, by contrast, operates in a manner that depends on precisely
what goods the government buys or what taxes and transfers it changes.
 •   An increase in government spending raises consumption spending along
with government purchases.
 •   An income tax cut has a direct effect on consumption spending.
 •   An investment subsidy   increases investment spending.
