Aggregate Supply and Demand
 Aggregate Demand Curve
•   Suppose money supply (the nominal money supply ) is M and the
price level is P the the real money supply as M/P.
 •   When M/P rises, interest rates fall and investment rises, leading
overall aggregate demand to rise.
 •   For a given level of the nominal money supply, M , high prices
mean a low real money supply, M/P.
 •   A high price level means a low level of aggregate demand, and a
low price level means a high level of aggregate demand.
 • Expansionary fiscal policy and monetary policy —moves the
aggregate demand schedule up and to the right.
