level of income has to rise.
 •   responsiveness of the demand for money to income - k
 •   responsiveness of the demand for money to interest - h
 •   higher the value of k and lower the value of h steeper the LM curve
will be.
 - Vertical LM Curve ,   h = tends to 0
 - Horizontal LM Curve , h is very high (sensitive)

 The Position of the LM Curve

 •   Change in real Money Supply cause disequilibrium
 •   the interest rate has to decline to induce people to hold the larger real
quantity of money
 •   At each level of income the equilibrium interest rate has to be lower
to induce people to hold the larger real quantity of money.
 •   at each level of the interest rate the level of income has to be higher
to raise the transactions demand for money and thereby absorb the
higher real money supply.
